# Boarding-school influenza, England, 1978

Daily count of boys confined to bed during the influenza outbreak at an
English boarding school, reported in the \*British Medical Journal\*, 4
March 1978. 763 boys, one index case, about two weeks of counts. This is
the series fitted in \`sd_example("boarding_school_flu")\`.

## Usage

``` r
boarding_school_flu
```

## Format

A data frame with 15 rows and 2 columns:

- time:

  Day of the outbreak, 0 = the index case.

- sInfected:

  Boys confined to bed.

## Source

Anonymous (1978) "Influenza in a boarding school", \*British Medical
Journal\* 1:587.
